Cohesion map

Reference this, that, it, they, such, which — point the reader back to an identifiable idea.
Substitution one/ones, do, so, the same — replace an item without repeating it.
Ellipsis leave recoverable material unsaid: “Maria chose the red folder; James, the blue one.”
Conjunction show relations such as contrast, cause, result, addition, time and example.
Lexical cohesion maintain the semantic field through repetition, synonymy, superordinates and collocation.

Coherence check

A paragraph can be highly cohesive yet incoherent. Ask: Does each sentence develop the controlling idea, and does the reader know why this sentence comes next?

Pinker edit

Agent test
Can you identify who performs the main action in every sentence?
Verb test
Can a weak phrase such as “make a reduction in” become a direct verb such as “reduce”?
Reader test
For every “this / it / they / which”, point to exactly what it refers to.

Essay architect

Plan the argument before you decorate it with transitions.
